Handover guarantee
The handover guarantee applies only to end-of-tenancy cleaning at a fixed package price and only to the cleaning work agreed in the order confirmation. Hourly cleaning is provided without a handover guarantee.

If your property management or landlord raises a cleaning complaint at the official property handover, Kraken recleans the areas complained of free of charge.

The guarantee applies if all the following requirements are met:

The property handover takes place within 72 hours after cleaning.
The apartment remained unoccupied and unused after cleaning.
You report the complaints in writing within 24 hours after the handover, with the handover report or photographs.
Kraken is granted access for the recleaning.

Recleaning takes place as soon as possible, normally within 48 hours after notification, in coordination with you and the property management.

The following are not covered:

normal wear, damage, discolouration, and limescale or rust marks that cannot be removed with normal methods without causing damage;
painting, repairs and filling wall-plug holes;
rooms and extras not booked, for example a balcony, cellar, external blinds or carpets;
areas that were inaccessible during cleaning.

Kraken attends the property handover only if this has been expressly agreed.

If you have third parties rectify the cleaning without giving Kraken an opportunity to reclean, Kraken does not cover those costs.

The guarantee concerns cleaning only. It does not replace repairs or clarification of tenancy-law matters.
